    Known scope & scientific limitations

    • Permanent water mask: ~40% of Class-1 pixels on steep mountain slopes (>20°) represent radar and topographic shadow rather than open water bodies.
    • Sentinel SAR / MSI revisit interval (6–12 days); not a real-time telemetry stream.
    • Steep topography radar layover & shadow actively masked using DEM look angles.
    • Damage counts reflect pre-event OpenStreetMap inventory density.
    • Pretrained AI models segment flood water only; debris surge mapped by physical SAR/optical change.
